About RozgarJobs

One clean list of jobs in Pakistan — government, private and remote — with the closing date and the original advertisement on every card.

Why this site exists

Job hunting in Pakistan usually means opening five or six websites a day, scrolling past pop-ups, and finding out about a government post after the last date has gone. RozgarJobs fixes the boring part: the jobs are collected every hour into one fast page that works on a cheap phone and a slow connection.

What you get on every job

  • The original link. Every card ends with “Apply on …” and goes to the site that published the job.
  • The closing date. Shown as a Last date chip so you can see what is about to expire.
  • A free poster. Each job has a picture with the full details, made for WhatsApp and Facebook.
  • Plain facts. Pay, qualification, age limit and vacancies where the source publishes them.

How we collect jobs

RozgarJobs reads public job feeds that allow reuse with credit (Arbeitnow, Jobicy, Himalayas, Remote OK, Remotive), official Pakistani portals such as the National Job Portal, and advertisements that employers send us directly. We never copy an advertisement and pretend it is our own: the card is a short summary, the poster carries the details, and the apply button hands you over to the source.

We do not republish full advertisements. Advertisements belong to the organisation that paid for them. RozgarJobs shows a short summary, credit, and a link. If you are the publisher and want a listing removed, write to us — see Contact.

Free for candidates, always

RozgarJobs is free for job seekers. No sign-up, no CV upload, no “premium” unlock. Employers can post their own job with their own banner — see Post a job. The site is supported by small ad slots and employer postings.

Stay safe

  • Never pay money for a job offer, an interview slot or a “registration fee”.
  • Government posts are advertised on official portals. Check the department’s own site.
  • If a listing on RozgarJobs looks wrong, tell us and we will take it down.

Common questions

What does “sarkari naukri” mean?

It is the everyday Pakistani phrase for a government job. On RozgarJobs those posts appear under the Government filter, with the department, pay scale (BPS), qualification, age limit and closing date.

Is RozgarJobs free for candidates?

Yes. Browsing, saving jobs, alerts and posters are free, and we never charge for an application.

Where do the jobs come from?

Public job feeds that allow reuse with credit, official portals such as the National Job Portal, and advertisements employers send us directly. Every card links to the original advertisement.

How the site is built

RozgarJobs is a static website on GitHub Pages. A scheduled job finds new listings every hour, builds one page per job, and regenerates the sitemaps. There is no database, no account system and no tracker on the page.
