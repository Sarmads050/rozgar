Common sawal

Alerts free hain?

Haan. Rozgar pe candidate se kabhi paisa nahi liya jata — na alert ke liye, na apply ke liye.

Kitne emails aayenge?

Roz default ek digest rozana aata hai. Aap "instant" chunte hain to har nayi matching job par alag email aayega — lekin phir bhi din mein zyada se zyada 3.

Kya mera number sab ko dikhega?

Nahi. WhatsApp channel "follow-only" hai — aapka number kisi subscriber ko nazar nahi aata, aur aapke messages channel par post nahi hote.

Alert band kaise karun?

Har email ke neeche unsubscribe link hai. WhatsApp channel se sirf "Unfollow" dabaein.

Sirf ek shahar ki jobs chahiye?

Form mein city chunein — filter usi shahar (aur remote jobs) tak mahdood rahega.
