Common questions

Are the alerts free?

Yes. Rozgar never charges candidates — not for alerts, not for applications.

How many emails will I get?

One digest a day by default. If you choose instant alerts you will still get at most three emails a day.

Will other people see my number?

No. The WhatsApp channel is follow-only. Your number is never shown to other subscribers and you cannot post in it.

How do I stop the alerts?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email, or unfollow the channel.

Can I get jobs from one city only?

Yes. Pick your city in the form — you will get that city plus remote jobs.
