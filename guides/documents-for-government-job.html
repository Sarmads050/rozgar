Documents you need for a government job

Most rejections at the verification stage are not about marks — they are about a missing document or a name that does not match. Keep this list ready once and every later application gets easier.

The core set

  • CNIC — front and back, scanned clearly. Your name and your father's name must match every certificate you submit.
  • Domicile certificate — issued by the district. Required for provincial quota posts.
  • Matric certificate and, where applicable, intermediate, bachelor's and master's certificates plus transcripts.
  • Experience letters from each previous employer, on letterhead, with dates and the job title. A CV is not a substitute.
  • Photograph — recent, plain background, in the size the advertisement asks for.
  • Character certificate — some departments ask for one from your last institution or employer.
  • Medical certificate — only where the advertisement requires it.

Attestation

Many advertisements ask for attested copies rather than originals, and they name who may attest them — usually a gazetted officer, a head of institution or a notary. Read that line before you pay anyone, because the accepted authority differs between departments.

Names must match. If your matric certificate spells your name differently from your CNIC, fix the discrepancy or attach the affidavit the advertisement allows. This one issue causes a large share of verification problems.

For the test and interview

  • Original documents plus one photocopy set.
  • The call letter or admit card, printed.
  • Original CNIC — you will not be allowed in without it.
  • Two or three extra photographs, and a pen.

How to keep it organised

  1. Scan everything once at a readable resolution and keep the files in one folder.
  2. Name the files clearly: cnic-front.pdf, matric-certificate.pdf, experience-abc-company.pdf.
  3. Keep one set of attested photocopies in a single envelope.
  4. Note every closing date in a calendar with a reminder two days before.

Never do this

Never hand original documents to anyone who is not the department itself, never pay a "processing agent", and never let a stranger keep your CNIC copies. If a listing asks for money or originals before an interview, report it to us.

Updated 23 October 2026. Requirements differ per advertisement — treat that document as the final word.
