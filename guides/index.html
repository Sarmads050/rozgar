Why these pages exist

Most job boards list vacancies and stop there. But a large share of applications fail for reasons that have nothing to do with the job: a missing domicile copy, a name that does not match the CNIC, a form submitted a day late, or a fee paid to a person who was never a recruiter. These guides fix that part.
