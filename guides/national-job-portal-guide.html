How to apply on the National Job Portal (NJP)

The National Job Portal is where most federal ministries and departments now advertise their vacancies. This is the whole process in plain English, from making the account to checking the status.

What NJP is

njp.gov.pk is a single website used for federal government posts. Instead of sending a separate form to each department, you fill your details once and apply to many posts. Not every government job is there — provincial posts, army recruitment and most teaching jobs are advertised elsewhere — but when a federal department is hiring, NJP is usually the first place it appears.

Before you start

  • An email address you actually open. The activation link and every later update go there.
  • Your CNIC in front of you — the number has to match your documents.
  • Scanned copies of your CNIC, domicile, degrees and experience letters, as PDF or JPG.
  • One recent photograph with a plain background.

File size matters. The portal refuses uploads that are too large. If a PDF will not upload, scan it at a lower resolution or compress it before you try again.

The steps

  1. Create the account. Open njp.gov.pk, choose sign-up, and enter your CNIC, email and mobile number. Confirm the activation link that arrives by email.
  2. Build the profile. Fill education, experience and personal details. Everything you complete here is reused when you apply, so it is worth doing properly once.
  3. Upload the documents. Put each certificate in the matching slot and check that the scan is readable — an unreadable document is a common reason for rejection.
  4. Find the job. Search on the portal, or find the vacancy on RozgarJobs and open the original advertisement from the job page.
  5. Read the advertisement first. Qualification, experience, age limit, domicile quota and the closing date are all in it. Applying to a post you are not eligible for wastes your time.
  6. Apply and submit. Fill the form, attach what the post asks for, pay the fee if one is listed, and submit before the deadline — servers get slow in the last 48 hours.
  7. Save the proof. Download or screenshot the submitted application. You will need the application number if the department calls you for a test.

After you submit

Your dashboard shows the status of each application: submitted, under review, shortlisted or rejected. Shortlisted candidates are usually contacted by email or SMS, and the test schedule is published on the department's own website. Check both — a missed test date is rarely reopened.

Common mistakes

  • Applying after the closing date because the form was left for the last day.
  • A wrong CNIC number or date of birth — it can disqualify you at verification.
  • Uploading a degree when the post asks for a specific subject or grade.
  • Ignoring the domicile requirement on provincial quota posts.
  • Not keeping the application number.

Is there a fee?

Some posts have an application fee and some are free. The fee, the bank and the challan number are written inside that job's advertisement. Never pay a person who promises to "get you shortlisted".

Updated 23 October 2026. Portal rules can change — always follow the advertisement in front of you.
