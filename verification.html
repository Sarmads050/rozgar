How we verify every job

A job board is only useful if the vacancies are real. This page explains exactly what gets published, what gets rejected, and what happens when a listing is wrong.

Where the jobs come from

Every listing is collected automatically from one of four places, and each card shows its source:

  • Official government portals — the National Jobs Portal, provincial public service commissions and department notice boards.
  • Newspaper job pages — public boards that publish the same advertisements that appear in print.
  • Employer-submitted posts — sent through our post-a-job form and checked by a person.
  • Remote job APIs — companies hiring from Pakistan, used only for the Remote tab.

We do not copy advertisements from Rozee, Indeed, LinkedIn or Mustakbil. Their terms do not allow it, and the copyright in those adverts belongs to someone else. We keep the facts (title, employer, city, closing date, qualification) and always link to the original source.

What we check before publishing

  1. The employer exists. A named department, company or institution — not "a private company" or "a reputed organisation".
  2. The closing date is real and in the future. If no date is given, the listing is labelled accordingly rather than invented.
  3. There is a way to apply. A working portal, form, email or post address. A listing with no application route is never published.
  4. No money is asked from candidates. Any advert that mentions a registration, processing or test fee, or asks for CNIC photos and bank details, is rejected. Government tests charge a challan fee — that is paid at a bank to the commission, never to a middleman.
  5. No fabricated salary or benefits. If the advertisement does not state pay, the card says "not specified".

What we publish on each page

Each vacancy gets its own page with the position, employer, location, qualification, experience, age limit, pay scale or salary, number of posts, closing date, a short summary in plain English, and a link that goes straight to the employer. We do not host application forms and we never act as an agent for any employer.

Expired vacancies

Listings are removed automatically the day after their closing date, and the sitemap is rebuilt at the same time. If you follow an old link, you land on a page that suggests similar jobs that are still open instead of a dead end. This matters: Google removes expired JobPosting pages from its job search, and stale listings annoy the people who trusted them.

Corrections and takedowns

If a listing is wrong — a typo in the salary, a closed post still showing, or a vacancy that was never real — email hello@rozgarjobs.com with the page link. Mistakes are corrected within one working day, and an advert that turns out to be fake is removed immediately and the employer is blocked from future submissions.

How the site is run

RozgarJobs is an independent, self-funded project. It has no employer paying for placement, no paid ranking, and no affiliate links inside job listings. Running costs are hosting and a domain; if advertising ever appears it will be clearly marked and kept away from the job cards. Read more about the project, or see the privacy policy.
