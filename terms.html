Terms of use

By using RozgarJobs you agree to the points below. They are written in plain English on purpose.

1. What RozgarJobs is

RozgarJobs is a free index of jobs that other organisations publish. We are not the employer and we are not an employment agency. We do not take part in hiring decisions, interviews or salary negotiations.

2. Accuracy

Listings are collected automatically and can change at the source without notice. Always check the closing date and the details on the original advertisement before you apply. If a detail here is wrong, tell us and we will fix it.

3. Copyright and attribution

Advertisements, logos and posters belong to their owners. RozgarJobs shows a short summary with a credit and a link. If you own a listing and want it removed, contact us — publisher requests are actioned the same day wherever possible.

4. Employer postings

  • Post only real, open vacancies with a real employer name and a real way to apply.
  • No “work from home and earn 50,000 a week” schemes.
  • No listing that asks a candidate to pay a fee. That listing will be removed.
  • We may edit the wording, category or city of a listing so the board stays useful.

5. No guarantee of employment

RozgarJobs does not guarantee interviews, replies or jobs. Your applications go to the employer, not to us.

6. Scams

Never pay for a job, an interview or a “seat reservation”. If someone asks you for money in the name of a job you saw here, report it.

7. Changes

These terms and the site can change. The date below shows the current version.
