Contact us

Questions, corrections, removals and employer postings all come to the same inbox.

Email

hello@rozgar.pk — replace this address with your own in data/site-config.json (alerts.owner_email).

What to write

  • Remove a job — send the job page link and say whether you are the employer or the original publisher.
  • Wrong detail — send the job link and the correct closing date, pay or eligibility.
  • Post a job — see Post a job for the format; the first listing is free.
  • Security — see security.txt.

We reply within two working days. Takedown requests from a publisher are actioned the same day wherever possible.

What we cannot do

  • We cannot forward your CV to an employer — apply through the original link on the job page.
  • We cannot confirm whether a government post is genuine beyond checking the source link.
  • We do not charge candidates and we do not run a paid “guaranteed job” service. Anyone claiming that is not us.
