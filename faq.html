Job questions, answered plainly

The eight questions people ask most about finding work in Pakistan. Each answer is the short version — tap to open it, or read straight down.

Where can I find government jobs in Pakistan?

Government jobs in Pakistan are advertised by federal and provincial bodies such as the Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC), provincial public service commissions, the National Testing Service (NTS), the National Job Portal, and individual departments. RozgarJobs collects those advertisements, removes duplicates, and lists each post with its closing date, qualification, age limit and pay scale, so one page shows what is open today.

Is RozgarJobs free for job seekers?

Yes. Searching, filtering, alerts and RSS are free and no account is needed. RozgarJobs never charges a candidate and never asks for a fee, a deposit or bank details. Any listing that asks for money is not ours — please report it.

How often are the jobs updated?

Every hour. A GitHub Actions build re-reads each permitted source, drops vacancies whose closing date has passed, and republishes the site. Every listing shows the date it was posted and the last date to apply.

What does BPS mean in a Pakistani government job?

BPS stands for Basic Pay Scale, the national grading from BPS-01 to BPS-22. A higher scale means higher pay and more seniority: BPS-01 to BPS-04 is support staff, BPS-05 to BPS-14 is clerical and technical, BPS-16 to BPS-17 covers graduate-entry posts, and BPS-18 and above are senior officer positions. Each advertisement states its own scale.

What is the difference between FPSC, PPSC and NTS?

FPSC is the federal commission and conducts its own competitive examinations for federal posts. Each province has its own commission — PPSC in Punjab, SPSC in Sindh, KPPSC in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, BPSC in Balochistan. NTS is a private testing service hired by departments and universities to screen applicants; a post advertised through NTS is still a job with the department that advertised it, not with NTS.

What documents do I need to apply for a government job?

Normally a CNIC copy, matric and intermediate certificates, the degree and transcript for the required qualification, recent photographs, and the original challan or deposit slip for the test fee. Domicile and experience certificates are added when the advertisement asks for them. Keep attested copies and the originals together for the day of the test or the interview.

How do I know a job advertisement is fake?

Three signals: somebody asks you for money, the contact address is a personal mobile number instead of an office, or the advertisement is not on the department's own website. Government recruitment never takes payment from candidates. If a listing on RozgarJobs looks wrong, report it and it is removed the same day.

Can I find remote jobs for Pakistani candidates?

Yes. The Remote filter shows international remote vacancies that hire from Pakistan. Pay is usually quoted in US dollars, so check whether the role is a contractor arrangement and how payment would reach you before you apply.
