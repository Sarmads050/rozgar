      How do I find government jobs in Pakistan?

      Federal and provincial commissions advertise public-sector posts on their own websites and in newspapers. RozgarJobs collects those advertisements, removes duplicates, and shows each post with its closing date, qualification, age limit and pay scale — so you can check eligibility before you apply.

      Are the jobs on RozgarJobs free to apply for?

      Yes. Searching and applying are free, and no account is needed. Government recruitment never asks a candidate for money; if a listing asks for a fee, a deposit or bank details, it is not genuine.

      How often is the job list updated?

      Every hour. Vacancies whose closing date has passed are removed, so everything you see on this page is currently open.

      What is BPS in a Pakistani government job?

      BPS is the Basic Pay Scale, running from BPS-01 to BPS-22. Support staff sit at BPS-01 to BPS-04, clerical and technical posts at BPS-05 to BPS-14, graduate-entry posts at BPS-16 and BPS-17, and senior officer posts at BPS-18 and above.
