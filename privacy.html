Privacy policy

Short version: this site has no accounts, no tracking cookies and no advertising network. Your email is used only for the alerts you asked for.

What we collect

  • Saved jobs — stored in your own browser (localStorage), never sent to us.
  • Alert email — only if you subscribe. It is used to send the alert you requested, nothing else.
  • Server logs — GitHub Pages keeps basic request logs as the host. We do not receive or analyse them.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no passwords, no CVs.
  • No advertising or analytics cookies.
  • No fingerprinting, no session recording, no cross-site tracking.

Job alerts

When you subscribe, your email address and the city/keywords you chose are used to send you matching jobs. Every email has an unsubscribe link. If you use the WhatsApp channel, your phone number is never visible to other subscribers and we never see it.

External links

Job pages link to the site that published the job. Once you leave RozgarJobs, the privacy policy of that site applies. We use rel="noopener noreferrer" on every outbound link.

Children

RozgarJobs is not aimed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly keep data from children.

Your rights

Write to hello@rozgar.pk to see, correct or delete the email address you subscribed with. Requests are handled within seven days.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS by GitHub Pages. Content is rendered with strict escaping, a Content-Security-Policy limits what the page can load, and no secret is stored in the repository. Report a problem to the address above.
