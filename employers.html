How to publish your ad

Prepare your advert

Job title, company, city (or “Remote”), job type, salary range, a 150–400 word description, your apply link and contact email. Banner + logo optional but recommended.

Send it to us

Email jobs@example.com (replace with your address) or fill the submission form linked from the README. Attach the banner and logo.

We verify it

We check the company exists, the link is real and the salary/terms are honest. No fake jobs, no “registration fee” offers.

It goes live

Your listing gets its own page plus a card on the home page. You can ask for edits or removal any time.

Why we don’t copy other sites’ job adverts Downloading another company’s job advert, banner or logo and re-uploading it is copyright infringement and breaks the terms of sites like Rozee, Indeed and LinkedIn — it can get this domain blocked or taken down. Instead: we show a short excerpt of a public listing with a link back to the source, and employers publish their own adverts here. That keeps the site legal and keeps real candidates coming.

Posting rules

To keep quality high and the site safe:

  • No jobs that ask candidates for money, fees, deposits or bank details
  • No MLM, pyramid or “investment” schemes
  • Salary or a clear range strongly preferred
  • Apply link must be a real careers page, form or email
  • We remove any listing reported as fake or misleading
